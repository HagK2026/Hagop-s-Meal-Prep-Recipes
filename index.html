Breakfast

English Beef Breakfast Muffins

Foil-wrapped breakfast muffin sandwiches — make once, reheat all week.

5 portions 1 per workday

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Shape the patties

    Divide the beef mince into 5 even portions and shape into flat patties roughly the size of the muffins.

  2. 2

    Cook the beef

    Cook the patties in a pan over medium heat until browned and cooked through, a few minutes per side.

  3. 3

    Fry the eggs

    In the same pan, use an egg ring to fry the eggs into neat, muffin-sized rounds.

  4. 4

    Toast the muffins

    Split and toast the English muffins until golden.

  5. 5

    Assemble

    Layer each muffin base with a beef patty, a slice of cheese, a fried egg, and a spread of BBQ sauce, then top with the muffin lid.

  6. 6

    Wrap and store

    Wrap each sandwich individually in aluminium foil and refrigerate.

  7. 7

    Reheat

    Each morning, reheat one wrapped sandwich before work.

Notes: Assemble while everything is warm so the cheese melts slightly. To reheat, loosen the foil and warm in the oven at 180°C for 10–12 minutes, or microwave (foil removed) for 60–90 seconds. A pinch of salt and pepper in the mince goes a long way.

Chicken Fajita Rice Meal Prep

High-protein, cheesy fajita rice bowl — great for batch-cooking work lunches.

6 portions Covers 5 workdays + 1 extra

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the chicken

    In a large pan, cook the diced chicken breast with the fajita seasoning over medium-high heat until browned and cooked through.

  2. 2

    Add the vegetables

    Add the capsicums and red onion, cook until softened, a few minutes.

  3. 3

    Mix in rice and stock

    Stir in the cooked jasmine rice and chicken stock, mixing well until everything is combined and heated through.

  4. 4

    Melt the cheese

    Sprinkle the Mexican cheese over the top, turn off the heat, and cover with a lid for 2 minutes to let the residual heat melt the cheese.

  5. 5

    Finish and portion

    Drizzle with chipotle sauce and scatter over spring onions. Divide into containers for the week.

Notes: Source: Coachcutrona (social media reel). Makes 6 portions — one extra beyond the 5-day work week, handy for a spare day or a bigger appetite.

Slow Cooker Lamb Shanks

8 portions Freezes well

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oil

    Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat.

    Sear the lamb

    Sear the lamb shanks until browned all over, then transfer to the slow cooker.

  3. 3

    Soften the aromatics

    In the same pan, cook the brown onion, garlic, and celery until soft.

  4. 4

    Make the sauce

    Stir in the red wine, sugar, beef stock, and tomato paste.

  5. 5

    Combine

    Pour the sauce over the lamb shanks in the slow cooker.

  6. 6

    Slow cook

    Cook on low for 8 hours.

  7. 7

    Finish

Notes: Makes 8 portions (roughly one shank per serve). Since this makes more than a work week's worth, it freezes well — portion into containers once cooled and freeze what you won't use within 4–5 days.

Slow-Cooked Beef & Pork Bolognese Sauce

Rich, slow-cooked beef and pork ragù with pancetta, red wine, and a touch of milk and nutmeg.

3–4 portions Doubles well

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Crisp the pancetta

    Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the pancetta or bacon and cook for 3–4 minutes until it starts to crisp and release its fat.

  2. 2

    Brown the mince

    Add the beef and pork mince and brown properly until it gets real colour — don't stop at just grey. Transfer the meat to the slow cooker.

  3. 3

    Cook the vegetable base

    In the same pan, add the onion, carrot, and celery. Cook for 8–10 minutes until softened.

  4. 4

    Add the garlic

    Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

  5. 5

    Cook the tomato paste

    Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes to deepen its flavour.

  6. 6

    Deglaze with wine

    Pour in the red wine, scraping the bottom of the pan. Let it bubble for 3–5 minutes, then transfer everything into the slow cooker.

  7. 7

    Add the sauce ingredients

    Add the passata, crushed tomatoes, beef stock, milk, bay leaf, oregano, thyme, nutmeg, Worcestershire sauce, crumbled stock cube, and black pepper to the slow cooker. Stir well to combine.

  8. 8

    Slow cook

    Cook on LOW for 6–8 hours.

  9. 9

    Thicken (optional)

    For a thicker sauce, remove the lid for the final 45–60 minutes and cook on HIGH.

  10. 10

    Finish the sauce

    Remove the bay leaf, then stir through the butter and parmesan. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.

  11. 11

    Serve

    Serve with tagliatelle, pappardelle, rigatoni, or spaghetti. Toss the pasta and sauce together in a pan with a splash of pasta water before serving.

Notes: Serves 3–4 as written — doubles cleanly in a larger slow cooker if you want it to stretch further across the work week. Freezes well in individual portions; reheat gently with a splash of water or stock to loosen the sauce.

Quick Spaghetti Bolognese

A saucy, deep-flavoured stovetop bolognese — no slow cooker, ready in about 45 minutes.

4 portions Stovetop, ~45 min

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the pasta

    Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil for the spaghetti. Cook according to packet instructions, reserving a cup of the pasta water before draining.

  2. 2

    Soften the vegetables

    Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot, and celery and cook until softened.

  3. 3

    Add the garlic

    Add the garlic and cook until fragrant.

  4. 4

    Brown the mince

    Add the beef and pork mince, breaking it up as it cooks, until browned all over with no pink remaining.

  5. 5

    Cook the tomato paste

    Stir in the tomato paste and cook briefly to deepen its flavour.

  6. 6

    Build the sauce

    Add the diced tomatoes, passata, crumbled stock cube, Italian herbs, bay leaf, and salt and pepper. Stir well and bring to a simmer.

  7. 7

    Simmer, part one

    Simmer uncovered for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  8. 8

    Add pasta water

    Stir in the reserved pasta water to loosen the sauce and round out the flavour.

  9. 9

    Simmer, part two

    Continue simmering for another 15 minutes until glossy, saucy, and well combined.

  10. 10

    Combine and serve

    Remove the bay leaf and taste, adjusting salt and pepper. Toss through the drained spaghetti, then serve topped with grated parmesan.

Notes: This makes a generous, saucy batch — 1.1kg of tomato product (diced tomatoes plus the full 700g of passata) against 500g of mince. If it's looser than you'd like after the full simmer, just keep going uncovered in 5-minute bursts. Plenty of extra sauce here for freezing or more lunches.

Osso Buco Pasta

Slow-braised osso buco shredded through rigatoni in a rich red wine sauce.

4 portions Oven-braised, ~2.5 hrs

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Season the beef

    Season the beef osso buco generously with salt and pepper.

  2. 2

    Sear the beef

    Heat the olive oil in a large oven-safe pot over medium-high heat and sear the beef until deeply browned. Remove and set aside.

  3. 3

    Cook the vegetables

    Add the onion, carrots, and celery to the same pot and cook until softened.

  4. 4

    Cook the tomato paste

    Stir through the tomato paste and cook for another couple of minutes.

  5. 5

    Deglaze

    Pour in the red wine and beef stock, scraping up all the flavour from the bottom of the pot.

  6. 6

    Return the beef

    Return the beef to the pot, making sure the liquid comes most of the way up the meat.

  7. 7

    Braise

    Cover and place in a 175°C oven for around 2 hours, checking every 30 minutes towards the end. You want the beef tender and falling apart.

  8. 8

    Shred the beef

    Remove the beef and shred the meat, keeping the bones and marrow aside for garnish.

  9. 9

    Cook the rigatoni

    Cook the rigatoni, then toss it through the rich osso buco sauce and shredded beef.

  10. 10

    Finish and serve

    Finish with plenty of grated Pecorino or Parmesan and torn basil on top.

Notes: A couple of assumptions since the original didn't give exact amounts: olive oil for searing, 400g rigatoni, and a serving-sized amount of cheese — adjust any of these as you like. Serves about 4. Source: alisonkingg (social media reel).

Cadbury Chocolate Chip Cookies

Classic soft-baked chocolate chip cookies — quick, simple, and always a hit.

Makes ~18 cookies Bake ~10-15 min

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven

    Preheat the oven to 160°C fan-forced (180°C standard). Line two baking trays with baking paper.

  2. 2

    Cream the butter and sugars

    Cream the butter, brown sugar, and caster sugar together until light and fluffy.

  3. 3

    Add the egg and vanilla

    Add the egg and vanilla and mix until just combined.

  4. 4

    Add the flour and chocolate chips

    Stir in the flour, then fold through the chocolate chips until just combined — don't overmix.

  5. 5

    Shape the cookies

    Roll tablespoons of dough into balls and space evenly on the trays.

  6. 6

    Bake

    Bake until lightly golden around the edges, turning the trays once for even cooking. Cool on a wire rack.

Notes: Makes about 18 cookies. Rolling tablespoon-sized portions (rather than teaspoons) gives a better-sized cookie — if you go smaller, cut the bake time down to around 8–12 minutes. Keeps in an airtight container at room temperature for up to a week, or freeze the rolled dough for up to 3 months and bake straight from frozen (adding a couple of extra minutes). Source: Baking Envy (bakingenvy.com).

Mediterranean Israeli Couscous Salad

A Mediterranean pearl couscous salad with feta, olives, cucumber, and fresh herbs — great as a side or a make-ahead light lunch.

6 portions Make-ahead, keeps ~2 days

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Toast the couscous

    In a medium pot, combine the couscous with 1 tablespoon of the olive oil. Toast over medium heat, stirring frequently, until some pieces turn golden brown.

  2. 2

    Cook the couscous

    Add the water and salt. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer until the water is absorbed, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking.

  3. 3

    Make the dressing

    Meanwhile, whisk together the remaining olive oil, lemon juice, and garlic in a large bowl.

  4. 4

    Toss and cool

    Add the cooked couscous to the bowl and toss to coat in the dressing. Chill in the fridge to cool — a good time to prep the rest of the ingredients.

  5. 5

    Add the rest

    Add the feta, olives, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, herbs, and pine nuts if using. Toss together.

  6. 6

    Season and serve

    Season to taste with salt and pepper, adding a little extra olive oil if it seems dry. Serve immediately or refrigerate until ready.

Notes: Great make-ahead side or light lunch — holds well in the fridge for about 2 days, though the cucumber softens a bit after that. To keep it from going soggy, deseed the cucumber and skip leafy greens. No pearl couscous on hand? Orzo or acini di pepe work as a substitute. Source: Bowl of Delicious (bowlofdelicious.com).
